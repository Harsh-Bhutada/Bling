3. The 2-Minute Tape Measurement Method

1

Apply Clear Tape

Press a piece of clear scotch tape across the widest part of your natural nail bed, making sure it hugs the sidewalls.

2

Mark the Edges

Use a fine pen to mark lines on both sides where your nail meets the skin of your finger.

3

Measure on Ruler

Peel the tape off, place it flat on a millimeter ruler, and count the mm between the two marks. Enter them above!
